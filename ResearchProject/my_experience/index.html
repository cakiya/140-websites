My Own Experience

Online School during COVID-19 from WSAZ

Daily Life

Covid-19 started impacting my daily life once my high school switched to remote classes near the end of my first year. Most of my teachers also just decided that the rest of the semester was optional, so we basically got an early summer break! My daily life turned into waking up, playing video games, eating, sleeping, and then repeating. Once school started up again, sometimes I would literally wake up as class was starting and eat my breakfast right during my first-period class.

Academics

My academics fell behind during my junior Covid-19 lockdown year of 2020-2021 as I went from 4 A’s and 2 C’s to 2 A’s and 4 C’s. I struggled to keep focus in any of my classes and did super poorly in my precalculus class, which would bite me back when I took AP calculus AB during my junior year as I didn’t have any of the fundamentals to even understand calculus. The worst thing however, was that I did so poorly in my Japanese class that I decided to not continue next year out of fear that my GPA would be too bad to get into a decent college. It’s one of the only regrets I have about my time in high school.
