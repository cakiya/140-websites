Coronavirus from WHO

Introduction

COVID-19 was a pandemic that took the world by surprise in 2019, forcing a global lockdown for 2 years. Originating in Wuhan, China, COVID-19 attacks the respiratory system (that’s the system that the lungs are a part of) and causes symptoms to appear ~14 days after infection; ranging from none to fever and cough to lung/organ failure and death (Hao et all., 2022). Due to the highly infectious nature of COVID-19, countries began ordering citizens to stay indoors and limit social contact.

History

As COVID-19 spread, scientists discovered 5 different strains of the virus. Essentially, a different strain means a different type with different genetics; a strain might be deadlier but spread slower, or be milder but spread way faster, or be deadlier AND spread way faster! The 5 different main strains of COVID-19 were alpha, beta, gamma, delta, and omicron. Each strain was discovered 2-6 months one after another and each separate strain needed their own vaccine since a different strain would have different properties. (Hao et all., 2022)
